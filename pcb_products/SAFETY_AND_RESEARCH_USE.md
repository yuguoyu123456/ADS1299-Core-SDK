# Safety and Research-Use Notice

## Intended scope

Unless a specific hardware release explicitly documents otherwise, ADS1299 boards published in this repository are intended for:

- research,
- education,
- engineering development,
- prototyping,
- laboratory evaluation, and
- non-diagnostic biomedical signal acquisition.

## Not a medical-device claim

Repository publication does not by itself establish that a board is a regulated medical device, clinically validated system, diagnostic instrument, therapeutic device, or approved patient-monitoring product.

Do not use repository materials as the sole basis for diagnosis, treatment, emergency monitoring, or other clinical decisions.

## Human-subject research

Researchers are responsible for obtaining all approvals required by their institution and jurisdiction before collecting human data. This may include ethics / IRB review, informed consent, privacy protection, electrical-safety review, and data-governance procedures.

## Electrical safety

Biopotential acquisition can create electrical-safety risks if a participant-connected system is improperly powered or connected to other equipment. Users should perform an appropriate safety assessment for their complete system, including power sources, USB / computer connections, isolation strategy, accessories, electrodes, and any simultaneously connected instrumentation.

A PCB schematic or successful bench test is not a substitute for system-level safety engineering.

## Experimental responsibility

Users should independently verify:

- supply voltages and polarity,
- connector pinouts,
- grounding / reference configuration,
- current paths to participant-connected electrodes,
- isolation needs,
- component ratings,
- firmware configuration, and
- signal validity.

## Reporting problems

Potential safety defects, hardware errata, or misleading documentation should be reported through the repository issue process or the contact channel documented by the project.

This notice is intended to encourage responsible research practice and does not replace applicable law, institutional policy, professional engineering review, or product-specific compliance requirements.
