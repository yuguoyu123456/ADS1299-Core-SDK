## 8. Outcomes and student development

Describe evidence of skill development, research participation, follow-on projects, collaborations, internships, publications, or other outcomes.

Distinguish clearly between direct program outcomes and broader impacts that cannot yet be attributed to the initiative.

## 9. SDG-related indicators

Where relevant, report measurable activities that support the initiative's stated SDG alignment, for example:

- number of students receiving technical education or research support,
- number of resource-limited institutions represented,
- open technical resources created,
- health / biomedical research projects enabled, and
- cross-institutional collaborations established.

Do not describe these activities as United Nations-certified or United Nations-funded unless that status is formally documented.

## 10. Product / commercial relationship disclosure

If project-associated hardware, vendors, sponsors, or commercial entities benefited financially or provided in-kind support, describe the relationship clearly.

Grant recipients should not be presented as product endorsements merely because they received project hardware.

## 11. Problems, limitations, and corrective actions

- delayed awards,
- unavailable components,
- unspent resources,
- project withdrawals,
- ethics / safety issues,
- conflicts discovered after review,
- reporting gaps, or
- governance weaknesses.

State corrective actions where appropriate.
