# Biomedical Engineering & BCI Student Support Foundation

This directory is intended to organize a student-support initiative focused on biomedical engineering, brain-computer interfaces (BCI), open hardware, scientific education, and early-stage research participation.

## Mission

- Support students who are learning or conducting research in biomedical engineering and BCI.
- Encourage open science, reproducible engineering, and responsible sharing of educational resources.
- Support student participation in hardware prototyping, signal acquisition, data analysis, and interdisciplinary research.
- Promote equitable access to learning opportunities, mentorship, and small-scale research support where feasible.

## Planned contents

- `mission_and_governance/` — mission, governance principles, eligibility, review process, and conflict-of-interest policy
- `student_grants/` — future student grant or support programs
- `applications/` — public application templates and selection criteria
- `reports/` — transparency reports, funded activities, and outcomes
- `open_education/` — educational and open-science materials

## Transparency and independence

This initiative is independent and is not an official United Nations entity, programme, fund, partner, or affiliate unless such a relationship is formally established and publicly documented. The initiative may describe how its activities contribute to broadly recognized public-interest goals such as health, education, innovation, scientific capacity building, and responsible technology development, provided such descriptions do not imply formal United Nations recognition.
