# Biomedical Engineering & BCI Student Support Initiative

> Repository directory: `bme_bci_student_foundation/`

This directory organizes a planned international student-support initiative focused on **biomedical engineering, brain-computer interfaces (BCI), open hardware, open science, scientific education, and early-stage research participation**.

The long-term vision may include establishing a formally registered nonprofit or foundation if appropriate. Until such an entity is legally established, this repository should describe the activity as an **independent student-support initiative** rather than implying registered charitable status.

## Mission

The initiative aims to:

- Help students gain practical access to biomedical engineering and BCI research.
- Support student-led projects in EEG, biosignal acquisition, embedded systems, open hardware, signal processing, and related areas.
- Reduce barriers to entry by sharing educational resources, reference designs, development tools, and—when resources permit—small research grants or hardware support.
- Encourage reproducible engineering, open science, responsible data practices, and transparent reporting.
- Promote interdisciplinary collaboration among engineering, neuroscience, medicine, computer science, and related fields.
- Create opportunities for students from resource-limited environments to participate in research and technical innovation.

## What support may include

Subject to available resources and future governance arrangements, support may include:

- Small student research grants
- ADS1299 / EEG / BCI development hardware
- PCB fabrication or component support
- Open-source software and firmware resources
- Educational materials and technical workshops
- Mentorship and project review
- Reproducibility / validation support
- Student travel, presentation, or publication support when a future program explicitly allows it

No funding program is active merely because this directory exists. Each future call must publish its own eligibility criteria, available budget, review process, and award conditions.

## Governance principles

The initiative should operate according to the following principles:

1. **Public-interest purpose** — student education, research capacity building, and responsible biomedical innovation.
2. **Fair access** — eligibility and selection criteria should be publicly stated.
3. **Conflict-of-interest control** — reviewers and decision-makers should disclose relevant conflicts.
4. **Transparency** — funded activities and aggregate outcomes should be reported where legally and ethically appropriate.
5. **No pay-to-win selection** — applicants should not be required to purchase project hardware as a condition of grant consideration.
6. **Scientific responsibility** — human-subject, animal, clinical, and sensitive-data research must follow applicable institutional and legal requirements.
7. **Independence** — sponsorship or donation must not automatically determine scientific conclusions or award outcomes.
8. **Privacy** — public reporting should avoid unnecessary disclosure of applicants' personal information.

## Sustainable Development Goal alignment

The initiative may describe its work as **contributing to** or **aligned with** relevant Sustainable Development Goals, particularly areas related to health, education, research capacity, innovation, inclusion, and partnerships.

Such language describes thematic alignment only. It does not indicate endorsement, accreditation, partnership, affiliation, or recognition by the United Nations.

## Independence and use of names / logos

This initiative is **not an official United Nations entity, programme, fund, agency, partner, or affiliate** unless a formal relationship is later established and publicly documented by the relevant organizations.

The project must not use the United Nations name, abbreviation, emblem, logo, SDG branding, or other protected identifiers in a way that falsely implies authorization, endorsement, partnership, or affiliation. Any future use requiring permission should follow the applicable rights and authorization process.

Likewise, support from any university, company, nonprofit organization, government, donor, or international organization should only be described as a formal partnership when that relationship has actually been established.

## Legal-status notice

This directory does **not** itself create a charitable foundation, nonprofit legal entity, scholarship fund, tax-deductible donation program, or regulated fundraising vehicle.

Before accepting donations, issuing charitable receipts, making grants from a formal fund, or using a legally protected organizational designation, the project should comply with the laws and financial / governance requirements of the relevant jurisdiction.

## Relationship to ADS1299-Core-SDK

The student-support initiative is connected to the broader open-source project through education, hardware access, reproducible research, and student participation. However, grant or support decisions should be governed independently and transparently rather than functioning solely as a product-promotion mechanism.
