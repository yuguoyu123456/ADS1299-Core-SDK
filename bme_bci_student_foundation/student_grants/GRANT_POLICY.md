# Student Grant and In-Kind Support Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides a baseline framework for future student grants, hardware awards, component support, and other in-kind assistance.

No award program is active unless a specific public call is announced.

## 2. Forms of support

A future call may provide one or more of the following:

- cash mini-grants,
- ADS1299 or related development hardware,
- PCB fabrication support,
- electronic components,
- software / cloud credits when available,
- travel or presentation support,
- publication support, or
- mentorship and technical review.

The exact form and maximum value must be stated in each call.

## 3. Eligibility

Each call should define eligibility before applications open. Possible eligible applicants may include undergraduate students, graduate students, doctoral students, or other early-stage trainees.

Eligibility criteria may consider:

- current student / trainee status,
- field of study,
- project relevance,
- institutional or geographic constraints,
- available supervision,
- safety / ethics readiness, and
- whether the proposed work can be completed within the support period.

## 4. Evaluation criteria

Applications should be scored against published criteria. A suggested baseline rubric is:

| Criterion | Suggested weight |
|---|---:|
| Scientific / engineering merit | 25% |
| Feasibility | 20% |
| Student learning / training value | 15% |
| Reproducibility / openness | 15% |
| Resource need / access barrier | 15% |
| Public-interest potential | 10% |

Individual calls may modify these weights but should publish the changes in advance.

## 5. Ethics and safety

Projects involving human participants, animals, clinical data, personal data, or potentially hazardous experimental procedures must obtain applicable approvals before the relevant work begins.

Funding approval from this initiative is not a substitute for ethics approval, regulatory authorization, biosafety review, electrical-safety review, or institutional permission.

## 6. Product independence

Applicants should not be required to purchase a product from ADS1299-Core-SDK maintainers or affiliated sellers as a condition of eligibility.

Where project hardware is offered as an in-kind award, alternative approaches should not be penalized solely because they use other technically appropriate platforms.

## 7. Award documentation

Each award should record:

- recipient or project identifier,
- support category,
- award amount or estimated in-kind value,
- project title,
- support period,
- reporting requirement, and
- any applicable conditions.

Public reporting should respect privacy and applicable law.

## 8. Reporting by recipients

Depending on award size, recipients may be asked to provide:

- a short progress update,
- a final technical summary,
- evidence of prototype / analysis / educational output,
- lessons learned,
- disclosure of publications or presentations, and
- reusable open materials when appropriate.

Open publication is encouraged but should never override participant privacy, ethical restrictions, third-party licenses, patent strategy, or legitimate confidentiality requirements.

## 9. Misuse of support

Support may be paused or withdrawn when there is credible evidence of fraud, false eligibility information, serious ethical violations, misuse of funds, or material deviation from the approved purpose without reasonable explanation.

## 10. No guarantee

Submitting an application does not create a right to funding. Awards depend on available resources, program priorities, review outcomes, and applicable legal / financial constraints.
