# Conflict of Interest Policy

## Purpose

This policy is intended to protect the fairness and credibility of student-support decisions.

## What may constitute a conflict

A reviewer or decision-maker should disclose a potential conflict when an applicant or project has a relationship that could reasonably affect impartial judgment, including:

- current or recent supervisor / student relationships,
- close research collaboration,
- close personal or family relationships,
- direct financial interest,
- employment or ownership relationships,
- commercial competition,
- active disputes,
- direct involvement in preparing the application, or
- another relationship that could reasonably create perceived bias.

## Disclosure

Potential conflicts should be disclosed before substantive review begins.

A disclosure does not automatically imply wrongdoing. Its purpose is to allow the program to determine an appropriate mitigation.

## Recusal

When a material conflict exists, the reviewer should normally:

- not score the application,
- not advocate for or against the application during the decision process, and
- not participate in the final award decision for that application.

## Founder / maintainer conflicts

If an applicant, vendor, project, or award could directly benefit a project maintainer, sponsor, or associated commercial activity, that relationship should be documented and additional independent review should be sought when feasible.

## Gifts and inducements

Applicants should not provide gifts, payments, purchases, or other benefits to reviewers or decision-makers in exchange for favorable consideration.

## Record keeping

For each formal funding round, the initiative should retain a simple record of disclosed conflicts and how they were handled, while avoiding unnecessary public disclosure of private personal information.

## Questions of uncertainty

When unsure whether a relationship constitutes a conflict, disclosure is preferred. The program lead or an uninvolved reviewer should determine the mitigation.
